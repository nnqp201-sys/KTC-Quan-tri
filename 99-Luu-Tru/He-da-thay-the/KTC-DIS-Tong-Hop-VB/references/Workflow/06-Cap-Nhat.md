# 06-Cap-Nhat

## Purpose
Define how templates, prompts, skills, and workflow rules are updated over time.

## Steps
1. Collect feedback from real use.
2. Identify repeated errors or missing patterns.
3. Decide whether the issue belongs in prompt, skill, checklist, or workflow.
4. Update the relevant file.
5. Record the version and date.
6. Re-test the updated item.

## Outputs
- Updated asset
- Version note
- Change log entry
