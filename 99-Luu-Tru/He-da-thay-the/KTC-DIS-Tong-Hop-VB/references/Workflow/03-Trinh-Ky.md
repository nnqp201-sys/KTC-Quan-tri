# 03-Trinh-Ky

## Purpose
Define the approval-preparation workflow before submission to the signer or authorized leader.

## Steps
1. Confirm the draft passed review.
2. Attach required supporting documents if any.
3. Check whether the right signer and issuing unit are assigned.
4. Prepare the final version for submission.
5. Record what is being submitted and to whom.

## Outputs
- Final submission package
- Signer readiness note
- Attachment list
