# 04-Ban-Hanh

## Purpose
Define the issuance workflow after approval is granted.

## Steps
1. Receive approval.
2. Finalize numbering, signature, and issuance details.
3. Distribute the document to the correct recipients.
4. Confirm the issuance channel.
5. Record issuance completion.

## Outputs
- Issued document
- Recipient list
- Issuance log entry
